Privacy Policy — Ball Blitz

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Ball Blitz ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. By installing and using the App you agree to the practices described below.

1. Who we are

The App is published by WIXON.
Contact: wixonbb@gmail.com

2. Summary

  • The App does not require an account, login, name, email, or any sign-up.
  • The App does not ask you to provide any personal information directly.
  • Your game progress and high scores are stored only on your device; we never receive them.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect certain device and usage information, including your device's advertising identifier.

3. Information collected through advertising

The App uses Google AdMob (a Google advertising service) to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process data such as:

  • Advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID) and other device identifiers;
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, language, country/region);
  • Approximate (non-precise) location derived from IP address;
  • Ad interaction and app-usage data (ads shown, clicks, time in app), for the purpose of serving, measuring, and improving ads, and to limit how often the same ad is shown.

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. We do not have access to, and do not store, this information ourselves. Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the App presents a consent request, using Google's User Messaging Platform, before personalized ads are shown. You can choose whether to allow personalized advertising. Where required, ads are shown on a non-personalized basis unless you consent.

5. Data stored on your device

The App saves your game state, settings, and high scores locally on your device only. This data is not transmitted to us or to any server. Uninstalling the App removes this local data.

6. Permissions

The App itself requests only the INTERNET permission. The Google Mobile Ads SDK included in the App adds a few standard, non-sensitive permissions it needs to serve ads: network state access (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE), access to the advertising ID (AD_ID and the Android ad-services permissions), and WAKE_LOCK / FOREGROUND_SERVICE for background ad tasks. The App does not request any location permission and does not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, files, or precise location. Approximate location may be inferred by Google from your IP address, as described in section 3.

7. Children

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the advertising services, please contact us so it can be addressed.

8. Your choices

  • You can reset or limit ad personalization at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads), including resetting or deleting your advertising ID.
  • In the EEA/UK/Switzerland, you can change your consent choices through the in-app consent options.
  • You can stop all data collection associated with the App by uninstalling it.

9. Data sharing and security

We do not sell your personal information. The only data sharing that occurs is the automatic collection by Google AdMob and its partners described above, for the purpose of serving ads. Data transmitted for ads is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections by the advertising SDK.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at wixonbb@gmail.com.
